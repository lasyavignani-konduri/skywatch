Anomaly Detection Models & Rules
1. Unstable ApproachHigh / Weight 1
Triggered when aircraft is on final approach with high sink rate or excessive groundspeed.
distance < 12 nm && 200 ft < AGL < 2500 ft && (vrate < -1200 fpm || gs > 180 kt)
2. Holding PatternMedium / Weight 1

3. Rapid Altitude DropHigh / Weight 2

Metrics Formulation Guide

Disruption Pulse Index (0–100):
Quantifies aggregate terminal airspace disruption by weighting anomalies against total airborne traffic volume:
Disruption Index = min(100, (100 * ∑ w_i) / max(8, Traffic * 0.35))

Runway Efficiency Rating:
Calculated as the percentage of arrivals within 12 nm below 3,000 ft AGL aligned within ±25° of the runway centerline that execute nominal descent without go-arounds or destabilization.

Continuous Model Feedback Loop:
Every controller acknowledgment records confirmation (True Positive) or false-alarm tag, updating real-time accuracy: Accuracy = TP / (TP + FP) * 100%.
